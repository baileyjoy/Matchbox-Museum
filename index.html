01. Foundation

The Matchbox Museum is a digital collection inspired by vintage matchbox labels. The design uses warm colors, simple layouts, and classic typography to create a nostalgic feeling.

CSS Reset

A selective CSS reset removes the default body margin so the header fills the page. Border-box sizing makes padding and borders easier to manage. Images are also kept within their containers. Default list styles and keyboard focus indicators are preserved.

Other Design Tokens

  • Spacing: 8px, 16px, and 24px for gaps and padding.
  • Content width: 900px to keep sections readable.
  • Border radius: 6px for cards and buttons.
  • Shadow: A small shadow adds depth to cards.
  • Typography: Georgia for headings and Arial for body text.

03. Typography

The museum uses Georgia for headings because it has a classic, vintage appearance. Arial is used for body text because it is easy to read.

Illustrations and Advertising

Matchbox labels were often decorated with animals, flowers, advertisements, and colorful patterns. These tiny illustrations are an interesting part of graphic design history.
